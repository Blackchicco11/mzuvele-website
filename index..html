WHO WE ARE

About Us

Mzuvele High School, situated at 54 P Mkhiwane Road, Kwamashu, Durban, 4359, operates as a public secondary school within the P.O. Kwamashu urban suburb of KwaZulu-Natal Province, South Africa.

Mzuvele strives to maximise every learner's potential in order that she/he may serve society and experience personal fulfilment through the development of her/his unique talents and abilities. The school strives to maintain its record of proven academic excellence by promoting a sound work ethic, independence of mind, critical thinking skills and the confidence to confront challenges.

REGISTRATION PROCESS

Admissions

The admission to public schools like Mzuvele High School follows a structured process outlined by the South African Schools Act of 1996.

Timeline & Attendance

As stipulated, children between the ages of 7 and 15 are mandated to attend school. Registration for the upcoming academic year typically occurs between April and October of the preceding year, with specific age criteria for entry into Grade R and Grade 1.

Required Documentation

Parents or guardians are required to submit essential documents, including birth certificates and immunization cards. Non-South African citizens must provide additional documentation. Provincial Learner Admissions Coordinators are available to provide guidance throughout the process.

FINANCIAL POLICY

School Fees

Mzuvele High School is a public school designated as Quintile 3, catering to a community with moderate socio-economic status.

As a no-fee institution, there are no tuition fees for students attending this school.

This quintile level school is eligible to receive up to R1,602 per learner from the Department of Education to cover operational expenses and educational resources.

CAMPUS GUIDELINES

School Uniform

Mzuvele High School uniform policies are established by the school’s governing body, aligned with the regulations outlined in the South African Schools Act of 1996 and provincial Education Department guidelines.

These policies dictate the design, colors, and proper wearing of school uniforms to cultivate a sense of unity, equality, and discipline among students. For detailed information regarding Mzuvele High School’s uniform policy, individuals are encouraged to contact the school administration directly.
